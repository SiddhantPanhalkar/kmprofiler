# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want kmprofiler to be a welcoming and useful project for everyone. Contributors, maintainers, and users are expected to treat each other with respect, regardless of background, identity, experience, or point of view.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and patient.
- Discuss ideas and code without attacking the person behind them.
- Give feedback that is specific and constructive.
- Accept reasonable feedback and correct mistakes when needed.
- Keep issues and pull requests focused on the project.
- Respect other people's privacy.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following behavior is not allowed:

- Harassment, threats, intimidation, or discrimination.
- Insults, trolling, personal attacks, or deliberately disruptive comments.
- Sexualized language, imagery, attention, or advances.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Repeated unwanted contact after being asked to stop.
- Any other behavior that would be inappropriate in a professional open-source project.

## Where this applies

This code applies to project spaces such as issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other official communication channels. It also applies when someone is publicly representing the project.

## Reporting a problem

Report serious or sensitive incidents privately to the maintainer using the contact details on the maintainer's GitHub profile. Do not open a public issue if doing so would expose personal information or make the situation worse.

Reports will be reviewed as promptly and fairly as possible. Details will be shared only with people who need them to investigate and respond.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues or pull requests, reject contributions, issue warnings, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation.

The response will depend on the seriousness and history of the behavior:

1. A minor first incident may result in a private correction.
2. Repeated or more serious behavior may result in a formal warning or temporary restriction.
3. Harassment, threats, sustained abuse, or repeated violations may result in a permanent ban.

Maintainers will explain moderation decisions when it is reasonable and safe to do so.
